ABOUT THE GAME

TETR.IX is a modern take on the classic block-stacking puzzle game. Our goal is to create an engaging online community where players can enjoy competitive or casual gameplay.

Our Vision

At TETR.IX, we believe in bringing players together through simple yet challenging gameplay. We've crafted a familiar experience with modern features that encourage friendly competition and skill development.

Game Modes

CLASSIC MODE:
Play until the stack reaches the top. Speed increases with each level.

BLITZ MODE:
Score as high as possible in 2 minutes!

SPRINT MODE:
Clear 40 lines as fast as possible!

ZEN MODE:
Relaxing gameplay with no game over - perfect for practice.
