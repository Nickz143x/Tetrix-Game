ABOUT TETRIX

TETRIX is a futuristic reimagining of the classic block-stacking puzzle game. We've combined timeless gameplay with modern aesthetics to create an experience that feels both nostalgic and fresh.

OUR MISSION

At TETRIX, we're passionate about creating a stress-free gaming environment where players of all skill levels can enjoy the pure puzzle experience. Our zen approach removes the pressure while maintaining the satisfying challenge that makes the game so compelling.

∞

ENDLESS PLAY

No game over screens - when you can't place a piece, the board clears and you continue playing with your score intact.

⚡

MODERN CONTROLS

Precise movement with responsive controls, including hard drop, hold piece, and rotation in both directions.

🎨

VISUAL STYLE

Clean, futuristic design with customizable color schemes to suit your preferences.

THE TETRIX DIFFERENCE

What makes our version special:

• No pressure gameplay: Enjoy at your own pace without the stress of increasing speeds

• Clean interface: Minimal distractions so you can focus on the puzzle

• Customizable: Adjust settings to create your perfect playing experience

• Accessible: Designed to be enjoyable for both casual players and puzzle enthusiasts
